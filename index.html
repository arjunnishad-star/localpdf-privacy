Privacy Policy — Local PDF

Package: com.avtech.localpdf
Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how We handles information when you use the Local PDF Android app.

Documents

Files you open, scan, edit, convert, sign, or save are processed on your device. We do not upload them to a server we operate, and we cannot see them. Copies in My Files stay in the app’s storage until you remove them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall. If you tap Share, Android sends that file only to the app you pick. That app’s own policy then applies.

Account

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, phone number, or email for a profile. A password you type to open a file you are allowed to open, a watermark, or a signature you draw stays on the device.

Ads

Before some downloads, the app shows a full-screen Google AdMob ad. That uses the internet and the Android advertising ID. Google may receive the advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and whether an ad was shown. We do not receive your PDFs from Google, and we do not use any other analytics or crash-reporting tool. You can reset the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in Android settings. Google’s rules are in the Google Privacy Policy .

Permissions

Internet and network state are used to load ads, not to upload documents. The advertising ID is used by AdMob. The camera is used only when you choose Scan to PDF, and not in the background. Photos and files are used only when you pick or save a document.

We do not

We do not sell your documents or personal information, upload PDFs to our own server, or use your files to train a cloud model.

Children

Local PDF is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update the date on this page if the policy changes.
